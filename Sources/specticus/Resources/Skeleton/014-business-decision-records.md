# Business Decision Records

*Business Decision Records (BDRs) document important business decisions made during the project, including context, options considered, decision, and consequences.*

> **Note:** Individual BDR files live in the `BDRs/` folder (with status subfolders) and are referenced here or linked from relevant sections.

## Directory Structure

BDRs are organized in the `BDRs/` folder using the same structure as ADRs:

```
BDRs/
├── proposed/
├── accepted/
│   └── 0001-....md
├── deprecated/
└── superseded/
```

See the example in `BDRs/accepted/0001-business-decision-process.md`.
