# Architecture Decision Records

This folder holds **Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)** for {{PROJECT_NAME}}.

Place each ADR in a status subfolder:

- `proposed/` — under discussion
- `accepted/` — in force
- `deprecated/` — no longer preferred
- `superseded/` — replaced by a newer ADR
- `rejected/` — considered and declined

Use a short filename such as `0001-short-title.md`.
