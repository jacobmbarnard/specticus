# Code of Conduct

Participation in the specticus project—issues, pull requests, discussions, and
related GitHub spaces—should stay **professional, constructive, and on-topic**.

This is a **software project**. It is not a venue for interpersonal drama,
political campaigning, or social/personal disputes.

## Expectations

- Be civil. Critique **ideas, designs, and code**, not people.
- Disagree in good faith and on **technical** grounds when you disagree.
- Do not harass, threaten, dox, spam, or personally attack others.
- Stay on topic for specticus. Do not derail work with unrelated agendas.
- Prefer clear, accurate language so others can follow the discussion.

## Scope

These expectations apply to conduct **in this repository’s project spaces**
(and other project channels the maintainers designate). They do not rewrite
the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE) or limit anyone’s right to fork or use the
code under that license.

## Enforcement

The **original creator** and any maintainers they designate may, at their
discretion:

- Hide or delete comments
- Close or lock issues and pull requests
- Restrict or block participation

There is **no response-time SLA** and no formal investigation process. Handling
is practical and maintainer-driven, consistent with
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Reports

- **Conduct** in project spaces: contact the original creator or maintainers
  privately through GitHub when possible.
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do **not** use public issues for exploitable
  findings. Follow **[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)**.

## Summary

Be decent. Stay technical. Help the project—or step aside without drama.
